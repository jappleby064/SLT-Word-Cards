How do I search by sound?

Put the phoneme in Word initial sound or Word final sound, or press the button beside the box to open the built-in IPA keyboard and pick the symbol. Matching is on sound, not spelling — so ʃ returns words beginning like shoe whatever their letters. For the full set of symbols and their sounds, use the IPA keyboard and chart.

What does CVC mean?

The shape of the syllable: consonant, vowel, consonant. Cat is CVC, stop is CCVC, baby is CVCV. Type the pattern into Structure to filter by shape rather than by sound.

What do the four deck buttons do?

Download PDF builds an A4 file, twelve square picture cards to a page. Present deck runs them full screen, one at a time, with the word hidden until you tap. Test deck adds scoring and ends with the ones to revisit. Share deck puts the card list in a link.

Where can I look up what a word means or where it came from?

In Rosie's Dictionary. Search a word for its meanings and part of speech, how it is said in IPA, a diagram of the languages it came through, and synonyms you can search in turn.

Does anything about my clients leave the device?

No. The card list downloads once; every search, selection and PDF after that happens in your browser. No accounts, no analytics. A shared deck keeps its contents after the #, which browsers never send to a server. The dictionary holds its word list here too, and reaches outside only for a word it does not have, or for where a word came from. Full detail in the privacy policy.
