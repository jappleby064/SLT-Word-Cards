source page linked so the terms can be checked. Pictures are converted to a
500×500 JPEG — padded onto white, never cropped or stretched — a change of format
only, except where noted below.

**Two cards are still on an uncleared picture** and are not listed: `net` and
`rug`. Those have no licence on record and are not cleared to ship.
See [LICENCE-AUDIT.md](LICENCE-AUDIT.md).

credited below, but stay hidden in both apps until the row is set to `live`.

## The three altered pictures

`cat`, `sore` and `back` are the only pictures changed beyond reformatting. The
cat's flat terracotta background was keyed out, keeping the books. `sore` and
`back` are the same source illustration: both lost the blue ring around it, and
`back` additionally had the red pain glow removed and the torso flattened to one
skin tone, so that it reads as a person facing away rather than someone in pain. The Pixabay licence permits adaptation. It
also forbids redistributing content "on a Standalone basis … where no creative
effort has been applied and it remains in substantially the same form", which is
why both were edited and why neither is offered as a downloadable image alone.
